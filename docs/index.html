A benchmark for diagnosing agent failures

Score a diagnosis by what happens when you apply it.

When an AI agent fails a multi-step task, can another model find the decisive mistake and fix it with one action? ReplayRCA answers by replay: restore the world just before the chosen step, swap in the proposed action, let the same agent continue, and compare with a paired placebo.

No annotations, no LLM judge. Two tracks, post-hoc autopsy and live oversight, share the same score. Tasks come from OpsWorld, a deterministic, procedurally generated e-commerce back office with a multimodal level of QR codes and handwriting.

Two tracks, one score

Explain after the fact, or prevent in real time

Autopsy

Sees
The complete transcript of a run that failed, and the fact that it failed
Must
Name the decisive step and propose one replacement action
Scored by
Causal repair, in points of continuation success
causal repair = success(fix) − success(placebo)

Live oversight

Sees
The run as it unfolds, up to the irreversible action the agent is about to take; the outcome is unknown
Must
Before every irreversible action, let it pass or intervene once
Scored by
Net damage prevented over runs that will fail and runs that will succeed, minus 5 points per intervention
net = mean(effect − 5 × intervened)

  • The top is not separable

    None of the 78 pairwise comparisons is significant after Holm correction; the first model significantly below Grok 4.7 even without correction is DeepSeek V4 Pro, at rank 9. Split-half reliability of the ranking is ρ = 0.47, and separating two models 10 points apart with 80 % power needs about 84 items.

  • Flagships do not lead

    GPT-6 Astra ranks 8th and Claude Fable 5.1 12th. Claude models ran at high; an ablation shows max is worth +15.2 points to Claude Haiku 5.5 (p = 0.011), so the Claude ranks are lower bounds. Reasoning volume is only loosely related to rank (ρ = 0.54, p = 0.056).

  • The last-step shortcut fails

    GPT-6.1 Sol forced to choose the final step scores +28.1 against +45.2 with free choice (−17.1 points, p = 0.004). Scoring by agreement with a consensus step would rank GPT-6 Astra first and Grok 4.7 third (ρ = 0.43 with the causal score).

  • Scores replicate, diagnoses less so

    A second sample of GPT-6 Luna scores +33.5 against +37.1 (p = 0.53) and picks the same step in 74 % of items; a reworded prompt gives +30.6 (p = 0.16).

  • Hard items exist

    5 items are repaired by no model in any continuation; 35 % of items have a model-averaged repair below 20 points and 19 % above 80.

  • Images: opening beats seeing

    In 17 of the 33 level-6 failures the worker never opened the attachment. Most vision models lose 10 to 15 points at level 6, while the text-only DeepSeek V4 Pro (+42.4) and GLM 5.3 (+39.4) stay competitive by making the worker read the image.

How it works

From harvested failures to a replayed score

  1. Harvest. Small worker agents attempt generated tasks of OpsWorld. A failure becomes a candidate only if it recurs in at least two of three fresh attempts; API artefacts and step-limit failures are excluded.
  2. Calibrate. The worker's own action at its first checkpoint is replayed five times without any change. Failures with spontaneous success at most 20 % and successes with at least 80 % are kept, so that outcomes are stable under restart.
  3. Diagnose or supervise. Autopsy: the diagnoser reads the transcript (images included for vision models) and answers with a step and a sanitised replacement action. Oversight: the supervisor is asked before every irreversible action and may intervene once.
  4. Replay. The world is restored just before the chosen step, the action is executed, and the worker continues five times; the placebo does the same with the original action. Continuations are cached and shared between models.
  5. Report. Leaderboards with bootstrap intervals, paired sign-flip permutation tests with Holm correction, and the reliability of the ranking.

Guardrails

  • Deterministic worlds. Every world comes from a seed, so any state can be restored and every restoration is verified; fresh instances can be generated at will.
  • Paired placebo. Only the difference with the agent's own action counts, at the same step and through the same mechanism.
  • Anti-injection. Tools never return free text passed to them; in replacement actions, free-text fields are erased or truncated, so a diagnosis cannot smuggle instructions to the worker.
  • Equal budgets. The same 64 000-token output budget applies to every call; an answer that exhausts it is invalid and scores zero.

Code

Run it on your own agents

The environment, the pipeline, both evaluation tracks and the reports are released under the MIT licence. Python 3.10 or later; models are called through OpenRouter.

git clone https://github.com/jeremy1392/RCAreplay
cd RCAreplay
pip install -r requirements.txt
cp .env.example .env            # OPENROUTER_API_KEY=...
python -m replayrca.selftest     # generator, tools and checker, offline
python -m replayrca.harvest --workers deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017 \
       --templates late_delivery,recall --seeds 10-29 --difficulties 4,5,6 \
       --out data/items_all.jsonl --budget 10
